Open tables · how many, inside More

Round nine, third pass. The See all button is out. What stays is the thought behind 4a: the bar's More list is the one place the page already has for "the rest", so how many tables to show at a time can live there too, without a second category and without a heading. Two ways in: the amounts as rows in the list, like another game after the list's own line, with the chosen one marked as the bar marks the picked game; and lists that are not one long column. The More lists are held open here so they can be compared; untick the box to have them on hover only.

Zero · where this stands

Rulings so far Holger, 5 Sep

  • Two whole rows. Six tables, whole.
  • Most people never need more. The first six find them a game, so the way to the rest is for the few.
  • The rest is a picture, not a place, until it is asked for. No half-tables to click on.
  • Nothing loud at rest. Green only under the pointer, if at all.
  • Dots, the tray, and a See all by the bar go on 5 Sep, late. Pages, rails, hands, handles and hover controls are out.
  • The button beside the bar wins 5 Sep, night. It says See all, in a dark Evernote colour, 8px from the bar.
  • See all is out again 6 Sep. The amount goes into the More list, as 4a had it, but not as a second category with a heading. Either like another game, or a list that is not one long column.
  • 1b and 1e go on 6 Sep, later. But 6 tables is not obvious on its own; maybe headers after all. 1e to be built cleanly: 24px rows, a 28px strip, 24px cells, the right radius and line colour.

What this costs measured

  • A line of controls under the grid takes 28px plus 14px of air, on every visit, for everyone.
  • A side control takes 28px off the tiles' width, 9px each, and no height.
  • A control in the head takes nothing: the title row has the room.
  • A hover control takes nothing at rest, and is found by the pointer or the wheel.
