Title seam lab: the section title set into the rule

A static study for the frontpage mockup (index.html): the section title moves onto, above or beside the section seam, the way a book printer sets a chapter head into a rule with a fleuron either side. Every scene shows two seams from the middle of the page, a band-to-canvas seam (Trick-taking games) and a canvas-to-band seam (Rummy games), with ghost tiles above and below so the rhythm can be judged. The rule is the tile's own: 1px #D9D2C4 at a half-pixel offset inside a 14px layer, so it rasterises soft. The suits are the tile's own glyphs and sizes, in the inks the frontpage renders (grey 45%, red 50%, each with its inner rim). Nothing moves. Scene 1 changes nothing and is the control.

Reading the numbers: pad is the clear rule either side of the title; tick is a short piece of rule between the title's break and the nearest suit (0 means the suit shares the title's opening); suits cut their own 20px gaps as on the tile. Titles set into the rule have their x-height centred on the rule, so the paper boundary runs through the letters; the two papers are close in tone, and the "tab" variants show the alternative.
