Icon colors — our tiles vs the icon pack

Left half of each swatch is the icon's own painted background, sampled from the PNGs in Downloads/World of Card games 3. Right half is the color we chose in the mockup's cardroom palette. The chips say how close the two sit.

Across the 18 games, only 2 pairs agree (Spades, Hand & Foot), 3 sit close (Pinochle, Twenty-Nine, Crazy Eights), and 13 are different color families. The pack also has its own reuse pattern with no category logic: five wines and reds (Canasta, Double Deck Pinochle, Gin Rummy, Whist, Crazy Eights), three violets (Hearts, Twenty-Nine, Rummy), three greens (Euchre, Hand & Foot, Cribbage), three blues (Bridge, Spades, Go Fish).
