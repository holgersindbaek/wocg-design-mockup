Everything works: panels open (Esc or outside click closes, one at a time), the modal opens, the mode toggle switches all four tiles, and every action reports what it would do. Labels use the house vernacular, Join table / Play solo: the critique preferred opponent-contrast (Play online / Play bots) under the one-verb rule, but the rule's spirit is one PRIMARY per tile, and Join carries the multiplayer meaning with more scent than online does. The "..." is gone everywhere: research says the glyph is recognized but carries no scent and invites no exploration from a 45+ audience - a bordered, labeled "More ▾" or a scented text link replaces it.

What the research changed

Catalogs at scale: every studied catalog (cardgames.io, Trickster, CrazyGames, Poki, Netflix, Spotify, App Store, Steam, Arkadium, BGA) shows at most ONE action affordance per tile at rest; several show none. The calm comes from making the whole tile the target and letting rare actions live a level down. The "..." verdict: recognized as "more", but with no information scent; the fix is a labeled trigger ("More ▾" bordered, or "More ways to play" as a link), minimum 24px targets (44px preferred). Hiding: our rare actions (<10% of clicks) pass Nielsen's progressive-disclosure test - hiding is correct, only the trigger was wrong. Primary actions must exist at rest on all inputs; hover may change emphasis, never existence (iOS double-tap trap). Unforeseen issues now baked into every variant here: focus-visible mirrors hover, panels are disclosures with Escape-close and focus-return, one panel open page-wide, panels overlay instead of reflowing the grid, and the join pair never splits verbs.
