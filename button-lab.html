What the research says about split and segmented bars

Split buttons (primary zone + chevron menu zone) are a real, named pattern: they fit when one action clearly dominates and the menu holds closely related actions - which is exactly our data (Join is dominant, the rare actions are related). Requirements: a visible divider, a clearly separated chevron, and buttons that perform actions rather than navigate. The documented risk: the menu zone is a small, low-discoverability target, and the pattern suits experienced users more than beginner-heavy audiences - our 45+ guests are the caution case. Segmented controls carry the opposite advice: every guideline (Apple HIG, Material, GitLab) reserves them for choosing between views, not for firing actions - two actions in one segmented-looking bar risks reading as a toggle. So the honest form of the idea is the split BUTTON (v1), and the action-pair bar (v2) works only if both zones read unmistakably as buttons: bold labels, press states, a hard divider, no selected-state styling.
