Pattern notes

Material 3 names five levels (filled, tonal, outlined, text, elevated) and its core rule is one filled button per view. Duolingo's identity is the 3D pressed edge (border-bottom that collapses on :active). ElevenLabs runs monochrome pills where hierarchy comes from fill vs ring, not hue. The text-link secondary is the quietest honest pattern and suits low-frequency actions. Outline-primary flips convention - rare, print-like, works only when the page is calm.
