Dropup lab · the open interaction

Every tile runs the frontpage's green divided Play control. Each section varies exactly one axis while the others hold the research defaults: arrow flips up 150ms, panel rises 220ms from the button (the repo's own modal curve), trigger holds a darker fill with a stronger ring while open, contents are the research cut, geometry is a floating trigger-width card 6px above. Click everything - Escape and outside clicks close, one panel at a time.

The numbers behind the defaults

Open motion: six of eight systems converge on fade + small scale from a trigger-anchored origin at 100-250ms (Radix/shadcn 150ms zoom-95 + 8px slide; Base UI 100ms scale-.98; Fluent enter 250 decelerate / exit 200 accelerate; Headless UI 200ms; Bootstrap ships none; Material is the 500ms clip-height outlier with bottom-first stagger for upward menus and a 150ms close). Close should be faster than open. Transform-origin belongs at the panel's bottom edge at the arrow's x. Arrow: rotation = disclosure convention, static = menu-button convention; both defensible, flip wins for state legibility if the glyph is big enough - hence the inline SVG replacing the 10px PNG. Trigger: style the open state off aria-expanded, never latch the press-scale. Contents: 5-7 rows max, counts as trailing pills not label text, 44px rows, header only if it earns its line. Geometry: trigger width, floating with a gap; fused comboboxes fight the squircle. The one under-explored strong idea: dimming the sibling tiles while a chooser is open (Apple's background recede).
