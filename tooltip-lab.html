What the research says

What today's tooltip gets wrong, by 2026 standards. Global 88% opacity on the whole box (the single most dated tell), 20px bold for everything, a hard 1px ring plus a heavy 12px blur, cursor-following instead of anchored, no arrow, no show delay, and no dark mode. Every major product anchors the tooltip to its trigger, uses 11 to 13px regular text, one soft shadow or none, and shows after a short pause.

The current consensus spec. shadcn and Vercel: near-black chip, 12px regular text, 6 to 8px radius, no border, no shadow, a small rotated-square arrow, 150 ms fade plus zoom from 95%. GitHub: 11px on a #24292f chip, 0.1 s appear. Material 3: inverse surface, 4dp radius, no arrow, 150 ms in and 75 ms out. Linear and Notion: soft wide shadow, hairline instead of a ring, often a second muted hint line. Motion rules: enter 125 to 200 ms, exit faster, scale from the anchor with transform-origin, never from scale(0), strong ease-out like cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1). Delay: Radix uses 700 ms with a 300 ms skip window for neighbors; 250 to 500 ms is the common band. Arrows are optional; Vercel reserves the arrow for tooltips alone, so nothing else in the UI has one.

Dark mode strategies. Most systems bind the tooltip to an inverse token so it auto-flips (shadcn, Material, Carbon, GitHub). The brand-safe alternative is one constant-identity chip designed to survive both surfaces, which is what Figma ships and what the espresso ink chip does here. Frosted glass solves both surfaces with one spec at the cost of a backdrop-filter.

Migration is small. All 117 call sites keep working: the skin lives in one 14-line file (CustomTooltip.scss), and the engine already has node-anchored placement built in (positionTooltipAtNode, used for the touch path today). Switching from cursor-following to anchored, adding the 250 ms pause, and restyling the box touches CustomTooltip.js and its SCSS only. The one content change worth making: stop wrapping every label in strong, and let multi-line tooltips use bold for names and numbers only.

My take, round two. Within the picked family, C2: the capsule's chrome, the #d2cfca edge ring over the liftshadow, so the tooltip is one more piece of the family the stats capsule, category tiles, and lobby cards already belong to. C3's band fill is the fallback if any shadow feels heavy over game art, and C6 swaps the ring to the site's edge-deep divider token when the light ring gets lost. On text, C2's regular-with-bold-accents beats C5's all-bold in the stress test below, especially on the stats blocks. On motion, B6, the dropup damping, is the pick: it is what the tiles already do, and at 220 ms it stays snappy. One open question for the dark table: the cream chip glows on dark felt, so decide between keeping it (a lit card on the table reads fine) or giving it a dark twin from the same recipe (#262626 fill, #4a453e hairline, #F9F6F2 text).
