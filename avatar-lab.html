Avatar lab · the same faces, better on the felt

Every tile is a table: green-felt.jpg at 100px, 300 by 300, with the four avatars of the study at their seats at the in-game size (96px; 68 on a compact table; 48 on the frontpage's table tiles; 28 in the menu bar): the tambourine dog every new player gets, PiLady10's Supergirl, a bot's Tin Man and BobK the cowboy. The art is never changed: every tile is a render-time coat (a CSS filter, an SVG filter, or something drawn behind or under the face), so it applies to the win/think/lose variants and the Lottie animations as well. The Set switcher swaps in twelve hard cases (white fuzz, a felt-dark robot, a near-black penguin, a face whose skin and hair are the felt's lightness, the empty chair), the four faces' emotion variants, and the four legacy PNGs. Context adds the nameplates (over the chin, as in the game) or the opponent's hand behind the face. State shows the open-seat hover and your turn. The sight switcher runs the tiles through the colour-vision matrices, greyscale, a 4px blur (the squint test), an aged eye and daylight. Under each tile: the recipe, the reason, the risk, and the measured edge step: the luminance step across the silhouette's edge (0 to 255, measured with the faces alone on the ground, size and set you have chosen), the weakest face's lower quartile first, then the mean over the faces. Under about 24 the edge is not there; 40 and up reads at a glance.

The study, in short

Keep the structure the mockup chose on 6 September, change the numbers. A light rim inside the silhouette and a dark line outside is the only structure that holds on the felt (where only light separates the 43 dark-edged faces) and on paper and the light wallpapers (where only dark does). At 8% the rim is below measurement: white at 8 to 15% over the art's ink lands at the felt's own lightness, a dead zone, so the mockup measures like today on the felt and worse than today on khaki. At 35% inside (1px at 28 and 48, 2px at 96) and black at 60% outside (tile 14) the weakest face rises from 77 to 133 on the felt, from 41 to 111 on the twelve, from 93 to 152 on khaki, and the share of edge under 3:1 falls from .37 to .05. One SVG def and one drop-shadow string; it reaches the emotion SVGs, the Lottie frames and the legacy PNGs alike, and rendered the same in Chromium and Firefox.

The quiet version is 25% and 45% (tile 16), the first pair that beats today on every ground without a visible line on the light faces. The alternative with the most drawn character is the self-lit rim (tile 35): the eroded ring keeps the face's own colour, pushed lighter, with the same 60% line outside; same numbers, no white on skin or on the robots. That is the A/B against 14.

Nothing behind the face on the felt. Discs, chips, slates and team plates separate by brute force, are the loudest objects in the lab, and hide the free silhouette that only cardgames.io shares among card sites; a mid-green plate is 1.5:1 against the felt. A rounded paper square (tile 42) is right for the picker and the profile only. Size and baseline are the other half of one family: the pack rule (tiles 57 and 58) as a generated transform table, shipped second. States on the ring: the turn ring in the button's yellow with a leather line (72), the empty chair at 45% (73), the absent player greyed (74); bots framed exactly like humans.

Not worth doing: drawn outlines alone, the stickers, the ground-adaptive pair, the tone filters, the specular rim light, backdrop-filter, and every art-level edit. The full study, with the four research lenses, the census, the measurements, the panel and the implementation notes, is in AVATAR-STUDY.md.

The panel

Six judges, two per lens (craft and taste; legibility and sight; robustness and the table as a whole), each scored every tile from the rendered face sheets of five to seven states (62 to 95 sheets each; 5 = no better than today and the mockup). The total is the mean of the three lens means. Then three skeptics per leader tried to refute its place (14 of the 30 passes ran before the account's limit): the 35/60 keyline, the 25/45 keyline, the self-lit rim and the pack rule survived their legibility and engineering skeptics and were refuted by their craft skeptics on the 2px rim at 96 (a frost on the robot, chalk on the hoods); the empty chair's leader score was found to be borrowed from the keyline it rides on, and its ink ring was a plate under the chair (fixed since); the ground-adaptive pair is tile 14 pixel for pixel on the felt. Three judges reported the turn coats (67, 68, 72) as not rendering; they do, on the bottom seat, and the yellow band vanishes because the default avatar every new player gets is a yellow dog, which is the product judge's point.
