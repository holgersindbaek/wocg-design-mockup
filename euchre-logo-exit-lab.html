Euchre logo exit lab

Khang's own exit, kept and varied: the word becomes smaller and goes, with the motion it already has carried through instead of turned, fading from the first frame, in fourteen, ten or seven frames. Every tile plays his 13 Oct render up to the frame where the jack is gone, then his Spine skeleton takes the word over and plays one variation, authored as Spine keys.

Khang animates in Spine 4.2.43 (Esoteric Software's Spine; the skeleton JSON names the version). The 13 Oct folder holds the same Spine export as 1 Oct; only the video active.webm is new, so the new jack move exists only as a render so far. The tile marked Now is that render untouched, and tile 0 is its shrink rebuilt from frame-by-frame measurements of the video. Measured, his word is already rising when the jack goes under: its top edge holds while its bottom comes up, about 24 px a frame at the centre, and it shrinks a tenth a frame. His render keeps that up for two more frames, then turns and sinks for the rest while it eases out and fades over the last four frames; that turn is the jolt in the move, and any exit that starts by moving down puts the same jolt at the hand-off. The variations keep the speeds the word arrives with and let them run out: the word slows to a soft top, then sinks away faster and faster, flattening a little, fading from the first frame. Holger picked that sink with the fade on 13 Oct, then found the word slow to go once the jack has hidden; the same curves bent over ten and seven frames instead of fourteen make it snappier without a jolt at the start, since every version starts at the same speeds. Sink without the fade and Up and away (no turn, shrinking towards the top edge) were dropped the same day; they are in the design repo's history at commit 286d0a23. The numbered tiles keep everything up to the hand-off and change only what the word does next; the variations are animations in a Spine file he can open: euchre-logo-exit-lab-parts/for-khang/euchre-logo-exits.json (File → Import Data in Spine 4.2 makes a project of it, with its images beside it; the README there says the rest). Press Play on a tile, scrub its slider, or set the speed to ¼x to study a move.

How the hand-off works

The video's own last frame sits under every tile's scene, and on it the Spine runtime draws the skeleton with the jack's slots hidden: its own banner background, the word, and whatever an exit adds (the background is drawn in the same canvas so additive light blends on the felt as it does in Spine). The exit animation's first keys put the word where the video has it at frame 48. The numbers were measured in headless Chrome from the video (euchre-logo-exit-lab-parts/measure2.mjs). Each exit is one file in euchre-logo-exit-lab-parts/exits/, written with spine-keys.mjs; build-spine.mjs builds the Spine file and this page's data from them, and shots.mjs dumps an exit's frames as a contact sheet.
