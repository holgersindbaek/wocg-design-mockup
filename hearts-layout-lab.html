Findings

The ads and the system bars take more than any layout does. On a 393 by 852 iPhone the app’s page keeps two ad slots by the site’s own rules (a 100 banner at the top, a 100 sticky footer once the window is 760 tall), and with the status bar and the home indicator zone 30% of the screen is spoken for before a card is dealt. The playspace is 559 of 852 points. Worse, the site reserves the footer’s 100 on the app’s page but never places the footer there (the rule in AdWidget.scss skips data-platform="mobile"; the element is 0 tall in the capture), and its math leaves the 34 safe area above the footer, under the felt: 134 points of bare felt under the playable area unless Raptive’s script fixes a footer in. (The lab draws the footer directly under the felt and the safe area under the footer, at Holger’s word.) Giving that band back to the felt makes the table 693 instead of 559, a quarter taller, more than any version below wins by moving the people. The Ads switch shows every version at each setting.

Today the game gets 13% of the screen and the people 14%. In the pass state of version 1, your hand, the pass and the trick cover 13% of the whole screen, the avatars, plates, scores and fans 14%, the pills and buttons 2%, the boxes 3%, the ads and the band they reserve 23%, the system bars 11%, and 33% is bare felt. Your card is 70 by 98. The side columns are the cost: each is 86 wide (a 51 fan and a 70 plate), 44% of the width together, and the trick is squeezed into the 221 between them while 301 points of felt stand empty above and below it.

Recommendation

The recommendation of 30 September, version 11, went with the removed versions. What stands now is Holger’s own direction, built in steps from 12: the side seats turned and on the edge (16), everything on the edge (18), the top seat on a plate (19) or a row like yours (20), and the score board on top between the pills with the top seat under it (21, 22; 23 with 40 px plates). The lab leaves the pick among them to Holger; the measured differences are small (the trick 80 by 112 in 22, 82 by 114 in 19 and 21, 74 by 103 in 23) and the ad question still comes first: with the sticky footer gone the felt gains 100 points, with the banner gone too 200.

Questions for Holger

1. The ads. Does the app show a sticky footer at all? The site reserves 100 for it but never places it on the app’s page, so today’s app may carry 134 points of empty felt under the hand. The lab reads the site’s rules, not a device. Which slots should the app keep: none, the top, the bottom, or both? The switch draws all four; with Top the felt gains 100, with None 200.

2. The pills in 20. With the top row built like yours, Leave, Table, the bell, Friends and the account live behind the settings gear and the Play cell is the right button. Is that the menu, or do some of them need their own place?

3. The sizes in 23. The 40 plate is the desktop’s, so the lab pairs it with the desktop’s 24 px names and with 58 avatars (the plate’s width plus 18). Keep the phone’s 19 px names, or the 50 avatars, with the 40 plates?

4. Thirteen cards. In every version the hand stays inside your plate: on the 313 plate of the edge versions 21.2 pitch for 12 cards and 19.4 for 13, on the 297 plate of the others 19.7 and 18.1, where the index is cut. Should the pass state run past the plate instead?

5. The names on the side plates. A 10-letter name reads at 15 px and a 12-letter one at 13 in the 84 box; the ladder says half the players have 8 letters or fewer. Is 84 the box, or is a longer plate worth the trick’s width?

6. Landscape. The apps are locked to portrait, so the lab draws none. Keep it so?

7. Bigger names on today’s plates (1a to 1i). Three rules to settle: a floor and a fade (never, as 1a to 1c, or at 12 as the mix), a word broken with a hyphen (never, as 1b; under 14, as 1c; under 13, as the mix, which breaks 7% of the players’ names: Conquistador, Wunderkind, Dr Zimmerman), and a plate whose height follows its name (the top fan moves 16 down under a two-line plate). The tighter plate alone (1a) takes the share reading at 14 px or more from 48% to 75% and needs no layout change at all; the mix takes it to 97% and nobody under 12.
