The live counters

Every way to show “1,214 players online · 143 games live · 87 seats open,” researched and rendered live. The green dot stays; the question is the other two counters. All numbers tick like the real thing.

Research notes: the industry default is no icons at all (lichess, PokerStars, PlayOK: bold number, quiet word). No surveyed product shows three live counts — a third dimension is always an occupancy fraction, a clickable empty seat, or prose. The 16px icon test killed the poker chip (reads as a spinner) and top-down tables (blur into blobs); it crowned fanned cards with a pip, the filled spade, the cinema seat, the armchair, the dashed slot and the open door. The artifact currently defaults to stats-brief (one counter) — when you pick a winner here, we flip the full row back on.
