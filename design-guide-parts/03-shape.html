3 Shape, lines and lift

Guide 5.1 the radius ladder, 5.2 rings and borders, 5.3 lift. Every piece is the site's own markup under the site's own CSS, so the numbers are what a visitor gets. This browser draws squircles: checking. Every modal here carries modalInstant, the site’s own class for a box that opens without its entrance, since that animation fills both ways and would otherwise hold the panel’s centring transform. Without that support the whole ladder reads 6px wherever the token is read, a tray's track reads 8 round its 6px cells, and only the literal twelves stay 12. Chrome prints the two keywords as numbers, so squircle measures superellipse(2) and round measures superellipse(1). Where a piece from the about zone stands beside another piece, its column is clipped: that zone paints a band the width of the window, and in a lab well it would paint over its neighbour.

The radius ladder, the tray built

Every tray takes the tray rung, a pair cut to the 2px gap between the track and its cells. The track reads --radius-tray, 14: the control's 12 plus the gap. The cells and the thumb read --radius-tray-cell, 10.5: the track less 1.75 times the gap, because a squircle turns later than an arc. So the gap holds at 2px round the corner as it does along a side. Where corner-shape is unsupported the pair is 8 round 6. A hover at either end of the games bar fills to the midpoint of the pair, 12.25. The games bar's overflow list is a dropdown, not a track, and keeps the control's 12. The games bar and the modal's tray cannot be told apart.

The radius ladder, as round as the box goes built

Every small number here is half the height of the thing it is on, which is the round rung wearing a number: a chip and a tag are 16 tall and write 8, the waiting bar is 12 tall and writes 6, the award strip is 8 tall and writes 4, the modal's scrollbar is 6 wide and writes 3. Measured, a chip at 8 and a chip written as round draw the same pixels, since a radius is clamped to half the side and the squircle is kept. The values stay in the sheet, because a chip that ever wrapped to two lines would go pill-shaped if it asked for round. What changes is the shape, not the size: the waiting bar asks for corner-shape round and is one of the three true arcs in the design; a chip keeps the squircle.
