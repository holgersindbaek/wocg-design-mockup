Hover, press and disabled built

A press is transform: scale(.98), over 80ms on the frontpage's buttons with colour answering in 24ms; a modal's button declares no transition, so its hover and its press land at once. Disabled is opacity .45 with no pointer events.

Two buttons in a box, the foot built

A foot is display: flex with an 8px gap and 8px above it, or 12px when text sits above it. In a 400 box the two buttons share the width with flex: 1 1 0. They are spans, never buttons, because base.css styles a bare button.

The catalog's dropup built

The Play control feeds a paper out of its own top edge and squares its top corners while the sheet is out. The paper is a 1px lattice in a 12px squircle open at the foot: two top cells 48 tall, each an 18 / 20 label over a 12 / 15 sub-line, then six rows 28 tall at 14 bold, going to the tile's own pale tint under the pointer. These are the one pair of heights in the kit that is not a button height: 7.1 names them as menu items. Every tile dresses its dropup in its own game's colour, so the two below are the same paper in two inks.
