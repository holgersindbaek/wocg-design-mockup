The checkbox built

A checkbox is a 24px box, the height of a small button, with its word 16 / 24 bold 8px after it and 16px to the next. The box is a 12px squircle, the button's corner, in the field's paper and ring; picked, it is the action green with the accent ring and the pickers' tick, white over the ring's colour, at 20px. Picked, the fill lands at once and the tick draws itself from its start to its end over 220ms on the tray's clock; unpicked, the fill and the tick fade together over 220ms on the same clock, the tick shrinking as it goes. A press scales the box alone to .9, not its word. Holger picked the row of checkboxes over the tick chip on 22 Sep 2026. The reading page's current item keeps its Reading badge, the old chip's picked form.

The games bar built

The games bar is the same 28px chooser at a 12px corner, with 24px cells at 0 8px in bold 16 / 24 --ink-label, a 12.3px count after a middle dot, a 1px --line-tray divider, a green thumb with its ring and drop travelling over 220ms, a --line hover paper on an unpicked cell, no hand on the picked one, and a More cell whose list keeps the 12px corner. The cells are 8 apart rather than 10 so one more game stands on the bar before the fold (Holger, 17 Sep 2026), and the corner is the shared --radius since 22 Sep 2026.
