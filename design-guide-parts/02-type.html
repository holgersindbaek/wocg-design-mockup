The display scale built

GLCA takes one of five sizes: 38 / 1.15 for a page title, the hero alone with letter-spacing -0.01em; 26 / 1.2 for a section head, 26 / 26 on a seam; 23 / 30 for the about sub-head; 21 / 28 for an h3; and 19 at 26px for a question, 1.45 for a quote and 1.55 for the letter. 500 for heads, 400 for a letter or a quote.
