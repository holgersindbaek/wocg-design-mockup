Avatars off the felt and on it built

Off the felt an avatar wears the avatar line, a 1px dilation of its silhouette inked #252525, and where it floats free a rounded foot by clip-path; at the table and on a felt tile the foot stays square and the avatar wears the table's own shadow. Nothing sits behind the face.

Off the felt, left to right: a list row's face with the four drop-shadows every browser gets before the defs land, the same face with the filter, the bar's 28px face, and the settings picker's three tiles. The two modals and the picker are pinned to static and given a width, and carry the site's own modalInstant class so the panel's arrival animation, which would beat an inline transform, never runs.
