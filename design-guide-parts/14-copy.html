These rules are about words, so each card draws the real piece carrying the real site copy and measures the words themselves: what a button says, what a column is called, what order a tray reads in, and whether anything shouts. What a button says built

A button names what happens next: Pick another seat, Sign in, Reload game, Keep playing, Send. When there is nothing to do but read, Got it. Close for a panel of content you looked at. Done when a flow has finished. No button in the design says OK. Sentence case everywhere, no uppercase labels, no dashes as punctuation and no semicolons.
