Questions for you

  1. On a phone, should a toast in the box's column read 14/18 like the box, or keep 16/20? (Phone type)
  2. Should the toast be at least as wide as the box, or keep its own width? (Toast width)
  3. Should the gap between the box and the toast be 8px, like the box's buttons, or the table's gutter? (Gap)
  4. In the narrow column a long toast wraps to 4 to 6 lines and covers the top of the West seat for 3 to 8 seconds (E4, E8). Is that fine, or should a long toast go back to today's place?
  5. The lobby keeps today's place for the toast, under the menu bar. Is that right?
  6. A status line can come every turn (M3, M4). Is a small drop fine that often, or should a status line only fade?
  7. On a phone the side slide moves only 8px. Is that enough to read as a slide, or should phones move 16px?

How it comes and goes

Eight ways for the box to arrive and leave. Today is kept as it is, snaps and all. Every other one fixes the snap: a new message during a leave turns the box round from where it is. Reduced motion gets a 120ms fade with no travel.

When the words change

Five ways for the words to change while the box stays on screen. M2 (the table fills) and M4 (turn status) show them best.

Where the toast goes

Five places for the toast at a table. In B, C and D the toast in the box's column arrives and leaves with the box's motion, so the two read as one set. A keeps today's toast motion. E rises from below.

The events

Each event uses the real texts and the real timing. M1 to M6 show the box alone, E1 to E8 show the box and the toast together. Where a real time is too long to watch, the event says so.

How this is drawn. The felt is the real dev site: a Hearts bots table mid-hand, captured on 2026-09-23 10:55 UTC at wocg c2c276cc8 (lobby-build with that day's uncommitted work) by message-toast-lab-parts/capture.mjs, at each size, with an iPhone user agent on the phones. Scripts, the live message box and the toast are taken out. The message box and the toast here are the real markup, styled by site.css. Their place, their cap and narrowCap are the numbers Table.js wrote on the live page.

One rule is copied in. site.css was built at wocg 67f48802c, before the box went to 16/20 at every size on 23 Sep (pieces.scss:2562). Each frame carries that one rule until site.css is rebuilt. The phone rule (14/18 on a small playspace) is already in site.css.

A defect on the real site. Table.js hideMessage hands its callback to a Y.Transition.Sequence, and the Sequence drops it (Transition.js:828 merges its own cb over it). So today the box stays display:block at opacity 0 after it leaves, and the current message stays set. The capture saw it: after a hide the box was display:block, opacity 0, and when the bookmark tip cleared, a message that had been hidden came back. This lab models the fixed lifecycle for every new motion: the box goes display:none when it has left, and a new message during a leave turns it round with no snap. Today keeps the defect, so B under Today shows the hole it leaves. Any column placement (B, C, D) needs that fix first, and the five games that test the box's display must then ask a new isMessageShowing().

Left out. The open Play panel switch (B's rule to stand under the panel), and the Animations setting at Off, which plays the same calm version as the reduced motion preview.
