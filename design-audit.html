3 Shape, lines and lift

Guide 5.1 the radius ladder, 5.2 rings and borders, 5.3 lift. Every piece is the site's own markup under the site's own CSS, so the numbers are what a visitor gets. This browser draws squircles: checking. Without that support the whole ladder reads 6px wherever the token is read, and only the literal twelves stay 12. The radius ladder, as round as the box goes built

Every small number here is half the height of the thing it is on, which is the round rung wearing a number: a chip and a tag are 16 tall and write 8, the waiting bar is 12 tall and writes 6, the award strip is 8 tall and writes 4, the modal's scrollbar is 6 wide and writes 3. The values stay in the sheet, because a chip that ever wrapped to two lines would go pill-shaped if it asked for round.
