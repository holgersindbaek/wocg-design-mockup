The app's Play menu

20 Oct 2026. Holger: People know it's a euchre game and they don't need to see the x open tables. And we should link to the tutorial and the leaderboards as well somehow. This lab shows the menu today, then 11 ways to change it.

The phones are the Euchre app on an iPhone Air (420 by 912), screenshots of dev in WebKit. The menus are drawn on top in the app's own styles, read off dev. The tutorial is about 4 minutes for Euchre. Press ☆ Pick on the ones you like. The bar at the foot collects them for you to copy.

Today

What is wrong

  1. The game's name in the head. The player is in the Euchre app. The Euchre logo stands right under the menu.
  2. “6 open tables” in the head. Players do not need it here. The home's “Join a table · 6 open” card under it shows the same number.
  3. No tutorial. The only way in is the “interactive tutorial” link inside the Rules box. From the code, that link leaves the app for Safari (tutorial-entry lab).
  4. No leaderboard. On the home it is only a link in the same paragraph. At a table it is a button in the Table panel.
  5. The two corners do not match. Activity and Profile are white panels with a paper strip. The Play menu is paper with a bold title over a white card.

My pick: V2

The Activity panel's head, titled Play, over today's grid with one more row. It fixes all five points with the smallest change. The two corners then open the same kind of panel.

If the tutorial must stand out more, take V4 for new players only (V10). The Professor's peek at launch (TutorialPeek.js, on dev) already asks new players once. So the menu only needs a plain way back to the tutorial.

Notes

  • Where the rows go in code. The tutorial starts as the Professor's peek starts it: Y.wocg.GameSelectors.getGameSelector(gameid).tutorialClickHandler(null) (TutorialPeek.js). Its minutes are the MINUTES table in the same file (Euchre 4).
  • The board: TableBar.openLeaderboard() reads table.gameid, so it fails on the home, where no table is. The home needs its own call with the app's game: new Y.ModalWebview().show("https://worldofcardgames.com/euchre-leaderboard?text-only=true"), as the Table panel's button does on a phone.
  • The look: the panel's corner is 12 on the phone (dev computes --radius-lg as 12 there; 16 on a desktop). The Activity head is 33px: a 32px strip on --paper-page with a 1px --line under it, 16 bold. Today's Play menu is 300 wide on --paper-page with the white card inside it.
  • Not captured: the board in the app's web sheet. Dev has no /euchre-leaderboard page (the daily job writes the game boards on the server only). Dev's /leaderboards page in the sheet logs the home out (“The site is open somewhere else”). V8's board rows are made up.
  • The glyphs in V7, V9 and V11 are sketches. Real ones go through icon-lab-all.html first.
  • Rerun the screenshots and styles: node app-play-menu-lab/capture.mjs from Design/WoCG-3 (dev must be up). The page check: node app-play-menu-lab/check.mjs.
