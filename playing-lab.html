The Popular-games grid, rebuilt to match the frontpage exactly (squircle 20px corners, retina grain, BuloRounded, the real hover lift). Two reference treatments, then the four kept directions, each with variations. In every new variant the number is bold and "playing" is regular at the same size.

What the research says

Verified across Steam, Roblox, chess.com, Discord, Twitch, YouTube, lichess, Pogo, CrazyGames, Poki and Board Game Arena: every subtle treatment lives in one of two registers: muted naked text under the art (chess.com renders "215,261 PLAYING" at 50% opacity with no container; Roblox and Discord use gray caption rows; Discord's tiny green dot carries the "live" meaning so the number stays gray) or a small translucent corner tag on the art (the YouTube duration badge and Twitch viewer tag). Steam uses a third trick: naked text where the status-green color alone means live. Notably, no major product hides the count until hover (hover is for previews), so the hover variants here are genuine experiments rather than proven patterns. Most direct card-game competitors (Pogo, CrazyGames, Poki, BGA) show no live counts at all, and both Roblox's tile change and NN/g's social-proof research warn that small numbers backfire.
