Ziggurat studies

T10 and W3 pushed the way you asked: short heroes, the Z1 text block fixed top-left, and the staircase filling the space below and to the right of the words — the first step slipping in under the text itself. One 12px seam everywhere, no wide water, every base dead flush, every same-size stack banned. Six silhouettes.

X1 · The grand stair

Three steps rising M→L→XL to the bottom-right corner on one flush base, a small riding each of the two big shoulders at staggered heights. The first step slides under the text.
