Yahoo variations

Ten takes on Y1, tighter and shorter, on a cleaner size ladder. The pattern behind Yahoo’s sizes turns out to be (almost) the golden ratio: each step S→M→XL multiplies by ~1.62, L is the half-step, and the ladder’s additive property means small + medium + one gap = extra-large, exactly — which is why spans keep landing on each other without a grid. Ladder here: 150 · 243 · 309 · 405 wide, all 3:2, one 12px seam. House rules: no two same-size tiles stacked, no playing-count chips, cross-seams stagger.

Z1 · The compact waist

Y1’s anatomy at hero size: two tiles bottom-align to the waist, two columns hang below, every stack alternates sizes. The footprint drops from 933×784 to 660×600.
