Live tables · make it something you ask for

The page shows open tables you can join, then live tables you can only watch, as two full width sections one under the other. A tester did not understand the second one and thought it took up too much room. This lab asks what live tables looks like when it stops being its own section.

The constraint is that nothing new gets invented. Every control below is one the site already draws, which is the segmented bar over the open tables, the felt card, the corner button and the plain link. Each variant answers two questions. Does a newcomer still learn that watching is possible, and how much height does it save. The live section costs about 420px today, so that is the number to beat. The choosers work. Click Live in any of them and the cards swap.

What it costs today

Two full width sections, one under the other. The first one you can join. The second one you can only watch. They look the same, so the second one reads as the first one repeated. The open section spends about 690px and the live section about 420px. Every number below is measured against that 420.

Two sections now

The page as it stands. A newcomer learns that watching is possible, because the cards are right there with no empty seats. It costs 420px and one more heading to read.
